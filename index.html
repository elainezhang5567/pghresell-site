Is your item a good fit?

We're selective on purpose. It lets us get you the best possible price.
Here's what tends to sell well, and what we typically pass on.

Great items to bring us

  • Sneakers & Apparel
    Especially new or with tags
  • Electronics & Computers
  • Tools & Hardware
  • Vintage & Antiques
  • Sporting Goods & Memorabilia
  • Toys & Collectibles
  • Specialty Equipment

Items that are not a great fit

  • Glassware
    Fragile and costly to ship safely
  • Tableware & Dinnerware
    Similar shipping challenges and limited demand
  • Figurines & Knick-Knacks
    Niche market with inconsistent resale value
  • Artwork & Paintings
    Pricing and authentication require specialized expertise
  • Used Clothing & Shoes
    Without tags, resale value drops significantly

The process

How it works

1

Submit your item

Create a free account and tell us what you want to sell. Takes less than 5 minutes.

2

Get your item to us

Ship it directly to us, arrange a free local pickup, or drop it off by appointment at 5877 Commerce Street, Suite 212, Pittsburgh, PA 15206. We work around your schedule.

3

We list & market

Our team creates a professional eBay listing with optimized photos and descriptions, marketed to thousands of buyers.

4

Your item sells

When a buyer purchases your item, we handle everything: payment processing, shipping logistics, and customer service.

5

Get paid fast

We take our transparent 50% commission and transfer your earnings to your account. Track your listing in real time through your seller portal and request a payout anytime.

6

No sale? No problem.

If your item hasn't sold within 3 months, you decide what happens next. We'll return it to you or responsibly dispose of it. No strings attached.
